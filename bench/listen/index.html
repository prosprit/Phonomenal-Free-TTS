Same sentence, every voice. Pick a sentence, then press play.

Ratings are saved in this browser only. In blind mode, rate before revealing. The second tab has Phonon's default voice on all 30 benchmark sentences (numbers, dates, names, abbreviations). Scores for the other 9 models tested are in bench/results.
