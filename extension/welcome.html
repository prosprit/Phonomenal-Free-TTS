Phonomenal is ready

It reads text on any web page aloud in a natural voice. It runs on this computer, so nothing you read is sent anywhere.

  1. Select some words with your mouse.
  2. Right-click on them.
  3. Choose Read aloud.

Good morning! The weather today will be sunny and mild, with a light breeze in the afternoon. It is a lovely day for a walk.

Want it to keep going? When it finishes, press Continue reading and it reads on down the page. Then click any sentence to jump there.

Reading a PDF? Click the Phonomenal icon and choose Read this PDF. It highlights each sentence as it reads, and you can click any sentence to start from there.

Tip: to change the voice or speed, click the puzzle-piece icon at the top-right of the browser, then pin Phonomenal so its icon is always there.
